Quick guide

Three steps. No guesswork.

Use this checklist before opening the flashing interface.

Identify your board

Confirm the exact device variant, display size/resolution, and ESP32 chip. Don't rely on the product name alone.

Choose the right firmware

Use a firmware build made for that exact board. Check whether the release needs a bootloader, partition table, or only a main application binary.

Connect and flash

Open the flasher, connect with a data USB cable, select the correct file(s), and follow the tool's prompts. Never unplug during writing.

⚠
Firmware warning

Flashing can erase data or leave a device unbootable if the wrong image, offsets, bootloader, or partition layout is used. This landing page does not detect your connected board or verify a firmware file; use the actual flasher's checks and the firmware maintainer's instructions.
